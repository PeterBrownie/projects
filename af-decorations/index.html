Air Force Decorations and Ribbons

Every decoration and ribbon on the AFPC listing, with practical criteria for knowing whether it belongs on your record. Check what applies to add an award to your list and build your ribbon rack.

Answer six quick questions to hide the awards that can't apply to your service, such as campaigns from before or after your time, or deployments you didn't make. Skip any question you're unsure about. Nothing is hidden for a skipped question.

My decorations

    Criteria are summarized from the Air Force's Personnel Center Decorations and Ribbons fact sheets. The full AFPC text is included under each award. This is an unofficial tool: it doesn't verify entitlement. Your DD-214, personnel records, and AFPC make the final call.
